  2. booking-v3 hint — 33 reports, issue #2. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what it says, and the form reads it day first. Same symptom as the parser, but only through the form, only at booking-v3 tenants, and the parser fix does not touch it.

How the reports were placed

  • One id, one report: the export repeats some rows (uniqueById).
  • A report’s problem comes from its tenant — locale and booking form, src/lib/data/tenants/ — and its channel, as causes.ts does. Not from intakeTag: intake guesses from the reporter’s browser, and the table shows how often that guess is wrong.
  • The one-day moves are the ids in desk-notes.json. As in the desk’s view, they stay counted where they are filed (parser), so parser and booking-v3 hint add up to the ticket.

Why one ticket hid three

Decision 0001 merges on subject alone, and all three problems arrive as “wrong day” subjects. Subject matching found one ticket, so the desk counted one bug. Splitting by tenant and channel is what tells them apart.
